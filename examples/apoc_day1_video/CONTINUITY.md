# CONTINUITY BIBLE — Day 1 of the Zombie Apocalypse
Reference this at the START of every prompt. Drift = bug frames = QC failures.

## ART STYLE (LOCKED)
- HIGH-CONTRAST BLACK AND WHITE INK-WASH, like a Walking Dead comic panel.
- Rough hand-drawn ink lines, heavy black shadows, ink-splat texture, gritty.
- **BLOOD RED is the ONLY color accent.** Everything else is black/white/grey.
- Red appears ONLY for: MC's t-shirt, blood, neon OPEN sign, clock hands at the end, ketchup bottle, warning labels, the sticker smiley, the burrito is NOT red, zombies are NOT green, car lights are white/grey, streetlight is orange-yellow grey (NOT vivid red).
- 16:9, no onomatopoeia text, no captions, no watermarks.

## MC ANATOMY (LOCKED — NO DRIFT)
- MINIMALIST STICK FIGURE: round WHITE head, two simple BLACK dot eyes, simple black mouth line.
- Body: thin BLACK STICK LINES for arms and legs.
- **NO colored pants, NO brown trousers, NO jeans** — legs are always plain black stick lines.
- He wears a BRIGHT RED SHORT-SLEEVED T-SHIRT (shirt is his signature, always red).
- He starts with a WHITE PAPER APRON slung over one shoulder — he takes it off in beat01m04. After that, no apron.
- He never wears shoes (stick figure feet implied).
- EMOTIONS: wide eyes = terror, half-mouth-smile = dark comedy beat, blank stare = shock.

## STORE LAYOUT (LOCKED — SINGLE STORY)
- Single-story American STOP-N-GO gas station convenience store. One floor. No stairs.
- FRONT: big plate-glass windows across the whole facade + one glass door with push-bar handle and deadbolt + metal drop-down security bar. Red neon OPEN sign above door.
- INSIDE FRONT: REGISTER COUNTER runs across the right side. Behind counter: cigarette rack wall, hot dog roller grill, cash register, baseball bat propped behind counter (with "WORLD'S OKAYEST MANAGER" sticker), EMERGENCY RADIO under counter, phone.
- MIDDLE: aisles of snacks/chips/drinks, slushie machine, soda coolers along one wall.
- BACK: long dark back aisle leading to WALK-IN BEER COOLER at rear (glass door, latches FROM OUTSIDE). Hand truck parked near cooler.
- SIDE/BACK: storage room door with handle, can be wedged shut from inside.
- OUTSIDE: asphalt parking lot, 2 gas pumps, orange sodium streetlight (B&W/orange-grey, not red), occasional parked cars, road/trees in far distance. Single-story perspective always.

## TIMELINE / LIGHTING STATE (per beat — REFERENCE THIS IN EVERY PROMPT)
Whole episode = **7 minutes, 6:47 PM → 6:54 PM, DUSK**, sky darkening blue-grey.
- Beats 01–10 (setup through locking door, propping wet-floor signs): POWER ON. Fluorescent ceiling lights bright white, soda coolers glowing, slushie machine humming. Sky still blue-dusk outside.
- Beat 11 (first wave hits): power still on, building shakes.
- Beat 17 (THE POWER GOES OUT): ALL fluorescent lights, coolers, slushie machine DIE. Store drops to dim.
- Beats 18–32 (after power out): only TWO light sources — (a) FLICKERING RED NEON OPEN SIGN on emergency battery above door, and (b) HEADLIGHTS of the crashed car outside shining white beams through front windows, casting long shadows. Deep black shadows everywhere else.
- Beat 33m01: neon OPEN sign DIES.
- Beat 33m02: car headlights DIE (battery runs out).
- Beats 33m03–36: TOTAL BLACKNESS. Frame nearly solid black, only faint white dot eyes of MC, red clock hands at end. Sound-lines only.
MC's position by beat:
- 01–10: behind the register
- 11–16: backing away from counter toward middle of store
- 17–24: on the floor behind counter, phone then radio
- 25–30: standing up, walking down back aisle with bat, looking at cooler
- 31–32: in storage room sitting on floor
- 33–36: in darkness (we don't see him after lights go out, just shadows/silhouettes)

## ZOMBIE RULES
- FAST sprinters — 28 Days Later style, NOT slow groaners.
- Run with heads DOWN at ~45 degrees, arms pumping like runners, mouths open snarling.
- MILKY WHITE EYES, skin grey in B&W.
- Clothing: torn ragged shirts, bare feet sometimes, various wounds.
- BLOOD rendered IN RED, the only other color.
- They NEVER get inside the store in Day 1 (locked glass holds). They are always OUTSIDE pressing against glass/windows.
- The infected delivery driver is INSIDE the WALK-IN COOLER (separated by glass + door), NOT in the main store.

## SPECIFIC PROPS
- Hot dog roller: beat02 only (cold, off), mentioned again at end beat35.
- Baseball bat: picked up beat27, carried through beat30, on floor beside him beat31+.
- Wet floor signs: stacked against door beat10; useless comedy.
- Phone: beats20–21 (no service).
- Emergency radio: beats22–24 (red indicator light).
- Delivery driver hand truck: in back aisle beat26 onward.
- Walk-in cooler: glass door, metal outside latch, beer cases stacked inside, cold fog breath.
- Crashed sedan: crashed into gas pump, headlights on beat18–beat33m02.
- Motorcycle in lot: run over by horde beat07m02.

## MODERATION RULES (avoid blocks)
- GORE IS STYLIZED ONLY. Small red blood stains as BLOTCHES/SPLATTERS on shirts or walls.
- NO visible open wounds, NO dripping/streaming blood, NO exposed muscle/bone, NO torn flesh close-ups, NO faces squished/distorted against glass, NO faces twisted in agony at close range.
- Infected/driver eye-change is shown as blank milky white eyes, not graphic facial deformity.
- Monster/danger is conveyed through body language, silhouettes, speed lines, composition — NOT through graphic injury detail.
- Think "Walking Dead comic book suggestion of gore" — ink splatter, a few red stains — not photorealistic horror.

## THINGS TO AVOID IN EVERY PROMPT
- No colored pants/jeans/shoes on MC.
- No zombies inside the store.
- No two-story elements, no stairs.
- No scaling/cropping — native output only.
- No extra colors beyond black/white/grey/red.
- No daytime/sunlight brightness after beat01 (dusk → night).
- No text words in images (except small stickers, the OPEN sign is a logo not on-image text, clock numbers are OK as part of the clock).
