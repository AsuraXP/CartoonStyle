TITLE
Day 1 of the Zombie Apocalypse (I Was Working Register)

DESCRIPTION
I was seven minutes from clocking out. The hot dog roller was cold. I was thinking about a frozen burrito. Then a man ran into the parking lot — sprinting, bleeding, chased by things that moved faster than any human should.

A first-person animated survival horror-comedy in stark black-and-white ink-wash comic style with red accents. Fast zombies. Quarter-inch tempered glass. A delivery driver who didn't make it out of the beer cooler. A baseball bat that says WORLD'S OKAYEST MANAGER.
