TITLE
Night 1: Trapped in My Bedroom (Zombie Outbreak)

DESCRIPTION
It's 2:47 AM. Your phone died at 1. The desk lamp is still on. Somebody just walked into your apartment who isn't supposed to be there.

A second-person survival horror-comedy in B&W ink-wash stick-figure style with yellow/red/orange accents. The dead repeat. Silence is survival. Loudness is a weapon. The living knock three times.

Same fast-zombie universe as "Day 1 of the Zombie Apocalypse (I Was Working Register)" — different night, different room, different person fighting their first hour of the end of the world.
