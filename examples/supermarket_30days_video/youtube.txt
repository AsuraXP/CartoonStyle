TITLE
I Got Trapped in a Supermarket During a Zombie Outbreak (for 30 Days)

DESCRIPTION
It was 11:47 PM on a Tuesday. I was just buying toothpaste. The doors locked. The staff vanished. The PA said BIOHAZARD PROTOCOL. Outside, in the parking lot, someone was eating someone. I spent the next 30 days locked inside a MegaSave with a lobster named Gerald while the world ended.

An absurd dark-comedy survival story told in flat stick-figure style. Biohazard lockdowns, a hurricane-rated glass door, an unkillable lobster co-president, a National Guard plywood crew, a garbage-can signal fire, and the worst sign-making mistake of all time.
