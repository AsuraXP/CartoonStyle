TITLE
I Got Trapped in a Supermarket for 30 Days

DESCRIPTION
It was 11:47 PM on a Tuesday. I was just buying toothpaste and frozen pizza. The doors locked. The staff left. The lights went out. Nobody was coming back for a month.

A first-person survival story about being accidentally sealed inside a closed big-box supermarket for 30 days, told in flat stick-figure style. There's a lobster named Gerald, a failed battering ram, an island of lantern light in the dark, a backwards sign, and a twist ending that will make you check every window you've ever taped something to.
